Where to Start?

Answer these with the page closed: ① 100 rises 20% and then falls 20% — what is it now? ② Which real numbers solve x²=x? ③ A fair die is known to show an even number; what is the probability that it shows more than 3?

Check answers and pick a starting point

① 96 — if this surprised you, start with 00. ② 0 and 1 — if unsure, start with 02 (and 01 for proofs). ③ 2/3 — if unsure, start with 09. These three questions only locate a starting point; they cannot certify mastery of a branch.

The Branch Map of Mathematics

00

Arithmetic, Functions, and Preparation

Numbers and operations; Fractions and proportions; Powers and logarithms; Equations and inequalities; Functions; Trigonometric functions

01

Mathematical Foundations and Proof

Integers, fractions, and ratios; Sets and maps; Propositional logic; Proof methods; Set theory, axioms, and computability

02

Algebra and Structure

elementary algebra; polynomials; group theory; ring theory; field theory; commutative algebra; representation theory; category theory and homological algebra

03

Number Theory

divisibility and congruences; prime numbers; Diophantine equations; analytic number theory; algebraic number theory; arithmetic geometry

04

Linear Algebra

Systems of linear equations; Vector spaces; Linear maps; Matrices; Eigenvalues; Inner products and spectral decomposition; Multilinear algebra

05

Geometry

Euclidean geometry; Analytic geometry; Projective geometry; Differential geometry; Riemannian geometry; Algebraic geometry; Convex geometry

06

Calculus and Mathematical Analysis

functions and limits; differentiation; integration; real analysis; complex analysis; measure theory; harmonic analysis; functional analysis; nonstandard analysis

07

Topology

point-set topology; continuity and homeomorphisms; compactness; connectedness; algebraic topology; differential topology; geometric topology

08

Combinatorics and Graph Theory

Counting; Recurrences; Generating functions; Graph theory; Extremal combinatorics; Design theory; Discrete geometry

09

Probability Theory

Probability spaces; Conditional probability; Random variables; Distributions; Law of large numbers; Limit theorems; Stochastic processes; Stochastic analysis

10

Statistics

descriptive statistics; sampling; parameter estimation; hypothesis testing; regression; Bayesian statistics; experimental design; multivariate and nonparametric statistics

11

12

13

Models and validation; Mathematical biology; Economics and financial mathematics; Networks and social systems; Systems science; Data modeling

17

History, Philosophy, and Mathematical Literacy

Number systems and their extensions; Conceptions of proof; The axiomatic method; Infinity; Philosophy of mathematics; Mathematics education and communication
