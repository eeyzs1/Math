Curvature: How Sharp a Local Turn Is

Goal for this lesson: explain "Curvature" in your own words, then complete the transfer exercise below on your own.

Prerequisites: Algebra, coordinates, and basic proof. Try the worked example first; if the notation feels heavy, go back to the preparation module.

Intuition First, Then Symbols

A straight line never turns; a circle turns at the same rate everywhere. A circle of radius R has curvature 1/R. For a smooth plane curve, curvature measures the rate of change of the tangent direction per unit of arc length.

A Derivation, Step by Step

Circles of radius 2 and radius 10 have curvatures 1/2 and 1/10. A small arc of a large circle looks more like a straight line. For general curves, curvature must be defined with calculus — it cannot be judged by eye alone.

Pause and Try It

If the radius of a circle is tripled, how does its curvature change?

Show the solution and self-check criteria

The new curvature is κ_new=1/(3R)=κ_old/3. The unit of curvature is the reciprocal of a length — not a length or an area.
