One group of items for each of the 139 lessons, self-checked in the sense of can explain / can derive / can transfer: answer closed-book first, tick only once you can do it, and revisit after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days to redo. Ticks are stored only in this browser (localStorage); nothing is uploaded or written to the repository.

Backups work across both editions and browsers. Exercise text is not included.
