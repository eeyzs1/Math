Proportional Change and Percentages

Goal for this lesson: explain "Proportional Change and Percentages" in your own words, then complete the transfer exercise below on your own.

Prerequisites: The four arithmetic operations; beginners can start here. Try the worked example first; if the notation feels heavy, go back to the preparation module.

Intuition First, Then Symbols

A percentage is a ratio with denominator 100. A 20% increase means multiplying by 1.2, not adding 20 unconditionally; under successive changes the base itself changes.

A Derivation, Step by Step

After rising 20%, 100 yuan becomes 120 yuan; falling 20% then yields 96 yuan. The two percentages act on bases of 100 and 120 respectively, so they do not simply cancel.

Pause and Try It

A price of 80 yuan falls 25% and then rises 25%. What is the final price?

Show the solution and self-check criteria

80×0.75×1.25=75 yuan. In general, an increase of r followed by a decrease of r leaves a total factor of 1−r².

Self-check: Is the conclusion correct? Are the conditions stated? Can you explain the key steps without looking at the example? If you only recalled the answer, redo the lesson.
